# Navilens Course Plan

## Summary

We will place navilens posters around Amos Eaton in relavant locations. The choice of Amos Eaton is for mutliple reasons. The first is the indoor location meaing that printed navilens codes will not be affected by weather conditions. The second is the ease of accessibility for the creators of the course. Finally, Amos Eaton is a building with specifically confusing bathroom locations and given that Navilens Educational Tages specifically cater to specifying bathroom locations the building seems like a good place to test run this.

We will begin by placing the following tags:
 * Women's Bathroom - 105 mm & 210 mm
 * Men's Bathroom - 105 mm & 210 mm
 * floor numbers - 110 mm
 * exit - 210 mm

# printing guidelines 
 * do not alter code's margins (specifically do not remove either the black or white borders)
 * do not change original code size
 * print on matte materials/do not place behind glass
 * do not change colors of code (even saturation or cmyk values)
 * place codes in well lit areas (do not place in half shaded areas, or beneath bright light)
 * place the code right side up (top left is always cyan, top right is always magenta, bottom left is always black, bottom right is always yellow)
 * place on flat surfaces
 * place codes high up (above 1.8 meters or 6 feet) so that codes will not be blocked by passerbys
